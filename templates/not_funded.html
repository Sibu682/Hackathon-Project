Key Application Requirements

  • South African citizen or permanent resident
  • Combined household income of R350 000 or less per annum
  • Registered (or applying to register) at a public university
  • Valid South African ID number
  • Grade 12 / matric certificate

Application windows typically open in September each year for the following academic year. Check the NSFAS website for current deadlines and requirements.
