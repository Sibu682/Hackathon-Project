How to Query or Appeal Your Defunding Decision

ℹ
NSFAS Appeal Portal

Submit your formal appeal at: {{ nsfas_apply_url }}

{% if student.funding_status.appeal_deadline %}

Your appeal deadline: {{ student.funding_status.appeal_deadline }}

{% endif %}
  1. 1
    Gather supporting documentation

    Collect your academic transcripts, proof of registration, NSFAS correspondence, and any supporting documents such as medical certificates or affidavits for extenuating circumstances.

  2. 2
    Contact UNISA Student Finance

    Call {{ unisa_contact_centre }} (Mon–Fri 08:00–19:00, Sat 08:00–14:00) or email {{ unisa_finance_email }} to confirm the specific appeal procedure for your situation.

  3. 3
    Submit a formal NSFAS appeal

    Log into the NSFAS portal at {{ nsfas_apply_url }} and submit your appeal with all supporting documents attached.

  4. 4
    Follow up regularly

    Track your appeal status on the NSFAS portal. Keep records of all correspondence. NSFAS typically responds within 15–30 business days. You can also call NSFAS on {{ nsfas_helpline }} (toll-free) or {{ nsfas_phone_alt }}.

  5. 5
    Explore alternative funding while you wait

    Consider applying for the UNISA Student Financial Aid Fund (SFAF) as emergency support, or explore the bursary opportunities listed below.
