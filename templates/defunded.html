How to Query or Appeal Your Defunding Decision

ℹ
NSFAS Appeal Portal

You can submit a formal appeal at: {{ unisa_nsfas_appeal_url }}

  1. 1
    Gather supporting documentation

    Collect your academic transcripts, proof of registration, NSFAS correspondence, and any supporting documents (e.g. medical certificates, affidavits for extenuating circumstances).

  2. 2
    Contact UNISA Student Finance

    Call {{ unisa_finance_phone }} or email {{ unisa_finance_email }} to confirm the specific appeal procedure for your situation.

  3. 3
    Submit a formal NSFAS appeal

    Log into the NSFAS self-service portal at my.nsfas.org.za and submit your appeal with supporting documents.

  4. 4
    Follow up regularly

    Track your appeal status on the NSFAS portal. Keep records of all correspondence. NSFAS typically responds within 15–30 business days.

  5. 5
    Explore alternative funding

    While your appeal is in progress, consider applying for the UNISA Student Financial Aid Fund (SFAF) as emergency support, or explore the bursary opportunities listed below.
